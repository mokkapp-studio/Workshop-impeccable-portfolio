# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, weighted equally:

- **Hiring managers and recruiters** (design leads, product leads, talent teams) deciding whether to interview Xavier for a product/UX design role.
- **Prospective clients** with a product problem deciding whether to hire Xavier for freelance or project work.

Both arrive to evaluate fit: can this designer handle a problem like ours, and how do they think?

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for a product/UX designer. It exists to present real case studies clearly enough that either audience can judge the quality of Xavier's thinking and decide to get in touch. Success means a qualified visitor reaches out, whether for a role or a project.

## Positioning

Product design for complex systems: untangling dense workflows, B2B tools, and design systems at scale. The case studies should prove the ability to make complicated products understandable and usable. That is the claim a generalist UX portfolio can't truthfully make.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: **Spanish only**. All copy, metadata, and document language are Spanish.
- Content is case-study-led: each project tells problem, process, and result.
- Open decisions, not yet confirmed:
  - Exact display name (for example "Xavier Pascual" or "Xavi").
  - Primary contact channel (email, form, LinkedIn, CV download).
  - Which industries or domains the case studies cover.
  - How many case studies appear at launch, and in what order.

## Brand Commitments

- Carries Xavier's personal name. No studio brand.

## Evidence on Hand

- Several complete case studies exist (visuals plus written story: problem, process, result). They are not in the repository yet; paths are to be added when imported.
- No testimonials, client logos, employer names, metrics, or awards have been provided. Future work must not invent any of them. Outcomes and numbers come only from the real case studies.

## Product Principles

1. **The work leads.** Case studies are the product. Everything else exists to get a visitor into them and out to a contact.
2. **Show the reasoning, not just the screens.** The positioning is complex systems, so the constraints, trade-offs, and structure behind each decision matter as much as the final UI.
3. **Clarity is the proof.** A portfolio that claims to tame complexity must itself be effortless to navigate and read.
4. **Serve both readers without splitting the site.** A hiring manager and a client should each find relevant work and a clear next step quickly, without separate tracks.
5. **Only true claims.** No fabricated outcomes, logos, or quotes. An honest, shorter story beats an inflated one.
